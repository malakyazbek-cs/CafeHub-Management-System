# CaféHub — Café & Library Management System

A Java desktop application that brings **café operations and book library management together in one system**, developed as an academic project.

## About

CaféHub simplifies daily coffee shop operations through a centralized system for processing orders, managing inventory and suppliers, administering staff, and tracking customer loyalty.

Unlike traditional café management systems, **CaféHub integrates a book borrowing library**, allowing the system to manage book copies, borrowing records, due dates, late fees, and availability alongside regular café operations.

## Key Features

- **Point of Sale (POS):** Process customer orders, manage transactions, and generate PDF invoices.
- **Inventory Management:** Track products and ingredients, automatically deduct stock after sales, and detect low-stock items.
- **Integrated Book Library:** Manage books, borrowing, returns, due dates, late fees, and copy availability.
- **Customer Loyalty:** Award purchase-based points and organize customers into Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Diamond tiers.
- **Staff & Supplier Management:** Manage employee accounts, role-based access, and supplier records.
- **Automated Notifications:** Identify low stock, overdue returns, and relevant library activities.
- **Analytics & Reports:** Visualize sales trends, payment methods, product popularity, and supplier performance.

## What Makes CaféHub Stand Out?

**More Than a Café POS**

CaféHub combines two systems that are not usually found together: café management and book lending. Customers can enjoy café services while the system manages library operations in the same environment.

**Smart Database Automation**

Database triggers and procedures automate tasks such as stock deductions, loyalty point calculations, low-stock alerts, and book availability updates, reducing manual work and helping maintain consistent records.

**Loyalty Meets Library Management**

Customer spending and borrowing records support a tiered rewards system, connecting customer loyalty with library services and borrowing benefits.

**Complete Operational Overview**

From sales and inventory to suppliers, staff, and book circulation, CaféHub brings multiple management functions into one desktop application with visual reporting.

## Technologies Used

- **Language & Interface:** Java, Swing, AWT
- **IDE:** NetBeans
- **Database & Connectivity:** MySQL, Oracle Database, JDBC
- **Database Logic:** SQL, PL/SQL triggers, views, stored procedures
- **Database Modelling:** SAP PowerDesigner
- **Charts & Reporting:** JFreeChart, iTextPDF, Apache POI

## What Users Can Do

- **Administrators:** Manage staff, products, suppliers, customers, inventory, and library records.
- **Café Staff:** Process orders, record payments, and perform authorized daily operations.
- **Customers:** Participate in loyalty rewards and use the available book borrowing and return services.
